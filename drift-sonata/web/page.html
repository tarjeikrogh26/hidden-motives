Life sequencer

six lanes · one per oscillator · 32 bars · each lane its own playhead

Each oscillator gets its own random patterns over 32 bars for everything it has: a little out of tune, its shape, its place in the 3D room, its level, its filter, cross-modulation and the odd octave jump. They follow the bars when the chord line plays.

Patterns from AVANTGARDIAN ARP, played by its own voices and folded onto the chords the strings play. Choose them in the bar below; ⚄ picks one, ✧ makes a new one. Without ▶ it plays over the chord you hold.

Vox

· no words, a new voice

Hums, held vowels, bits of syllables, breaths and sung chords from all the singers, tuned to the chords and laid across each other into a voice nobody has. Each piece takes its own path through the room; now and then one is thrown into the echo or the big room. Choose a Style and a Space in the bar below.

Song line

intro · verse · chorus · bridge · breakdown · outro

A whole song: a mood and a row of sections, each with its own chord line and length and its own feel. The chorus opens up, the breakdown lets the drums go, the intro and outro stay far away. Switch Song on and it plays from its first bar; click a section to change it.

Chord line

Save, sessions & MIDI

your sounds · the sound, chord line, arp, drums and tempo together

The MIDI file has the chords as DRIFT SONATA plays them (folded out by Tension), the Sub-Drop and, when they are on, the drums and the arp, each on its own track. Sessions are kept in this browser. Inside Claude you are asked before a file is saved, and the MIDI comes in a zip.

Play with the mouse, or the computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K (Z and X move the octave). Rose keys are what you play, brass keys are what Tension adds.
