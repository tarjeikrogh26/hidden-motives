Life sequencer

six lanes · one per oscillator · 32 bars · each lane its own playhead

Each oscillator gets its own random patterns over 32 bars for everything it has: a little out of tune, its shape, its place in the 3D room, its level, its filter, cross-modulation and the odd octave jump. They follow the bars when the chord line plays.

Avantgardian arp

2,281 patterns · 777 sounds · follows the chord line

Patterns from AVANTGARDIAN ARP, played by its own voices and folded onto the chords the strings play. Choose them in the bar below; ⚄ picks one, ✧ makes a new one. Without ▶ it plays over the chord you hold.

The six-oscillator arp sounds: each oscillator its own sound in the 3D room and its own Life lane. (An AVANTGARDIAN voice moves round you as one, with the same orbit.)

Perc

five lanes · their own cycles · all round you

Five lanes of percussion, each on its own rhythm and its own cycle, so they drift against each other and meet again. Over 32 bars the lanes come in and step out. Each circles you at its own height and pace, each hit flies a little while it rings.

Lead & bass

a melody that answers itself · a bass that walks the roots

The lead sings a small motif and keeps answering it over the chords: chord notes on the strong beats, steps of the key between, long notes gliding into each other, now and then a phrase of silence. The bass follows the roots in seven patterns. Switch them on in the Melody channel below.

Piano

long, simple lines · now and then · far out in the room

A soft felt piano: each key starts with a real recorded note and rings on, on two strings a hair apart. It plays in passages, a few phrases of long notes answering each other over the chords, then leaves the room to the others. Every note has its own place and drifts while it rings, into a big dark room and a slow echo.

Guitar

a player who drops by · real phrases · moved to every chord

Real guitar, played from the recordings: short phrases (a pick, a run, a strum) cut from them. For every chord the guitarist reaches for the phrase whose notes fit it best, moved a few semitones at most, at a speed near the song's. He comes in for a passage, plays a little, and goes again, standing somewhere in the room.

ASMR

ear candy · now and then · right at your ears

Its own engine: grains of whispers, words and close foley, turned round, moved by octaves, stuttered, never played as they were recorded. They come in short flurries (flutter, crawl, cloud, crackle, glitch, swarm), right round your head, then leave the music alone. Switch it on next to Perc in the bar below.

World

cümbüş · oud · ruan · santoor · sarod · jaltarang · pku · zurna · violin · another way every 32 bars

A player with many instruments, playing phrases from the recordings, each moved to fit the chord. He thinks in 32-bar stretches and, when he wanders, takes another instrument and another way of playing for each: a free taqsim, a hypnotic ostinato, call and answer, a soft tremolo bloom, or a duet. Switch him on next to Guitar in the bar below.

Synth bass

a modular analog voice · ladder · twin peaks · west coast fold · on the beat

One voice built like a big modular or a classic mono: several oscillators a few cents apart, a sub and noise, a complex oscillator for FM, a ring modulator, a wavefolder, drive and a crusher for the sour, scratchy ones, then a ladder, twin-peak, smooth, notch or low-pass-gate filter, each with its envelope. Its sequencer plays sixteenths on the beat from the chord, with accents and slides, and comes in a few notes at a time. Switch it on next to Piano in the bar below.

Vox

· no words, a new voice

Hums, held vowels, bits of syllables, breaths and sung chords from all the singers, tuned to the chords and laid across each other into a voice nobody has. Each piece takes its own path through the room; now and then one is thrown into the echo or the big room. Choose a Style and a Space in the bar below.

Singers

Song line

intro · verse · chorus · bridge · breakdown · outro

A whole song: a mood and a row of sections, each with its own chord line and length and its own feel. The chorus opens up, the breakdown lets the drums go, the intro and outro stay far away. Switch Song on and it plays from its first bar; click a section to change it.

Chord line

Save, sessions & MIDI

your sounds · the sound, chord line, arp, drums and tempo together

The MIDI file has the chords as DRIFT SONATA plays them (folded out by Tension), the Sub-Drop and, when they are on, the drums and the arp, each on its own track. Sessions are kept in this browser. Inside Claude you are asked before a file is saved, and the MIDI comes in a zip.

Play with the mouse, or the computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K (Z and X move the octave). Rose keys are what you play, brass keys are what Tension adds.
